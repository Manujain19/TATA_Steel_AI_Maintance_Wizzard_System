# Failure Analysis Report Extracts

## FAR-001 HRM-ROLL-01 bearing vibration

Observation: repeated drive-side vibration alarms appeared after coil threading.
Surface chatter and roll gap variation were observed on affected coils.

Analysis: vibration was consistent with bearing wear aggravated by low
lubrication flow. Roll alignment and chock clamp torque should be checked after
restoring lubrication.

Recommendation: reduce speed, inspect lubrication, plan bearing inspection, and
replace the bearing if vibration remains above warning limit after stabilization.

## FAR-002 HRM-COIL-03 mandrel expansion fault

Observation: mandrel expansion confirmation failed twice and tail-end slip was
reported by operations.

Analysis: the pattern indicates hydraulic valve leakage or worn mandrel
segments. The failure mode is critical because it can cause coil instability and
rejection.

Recommendation: hold production when the fault repeats, inspect the expansion
valve, verify pressure locally, and escalate mandrel segment procurement.
