# Steel Plant Maintenance Knowledge Base

## HRM-ROLL-01 finishing mill work roll assembly

Criticality: high

Common symptoms:
- High vibration near the drive-side bearing.
- Roll gap variation during high-speed passes.
- Surface chatter marks on finished strip.
- Rising bearing temperature after coil threading.

Likely causes:
- Work roll bearing wear or lubrication starvation.
- Mill stand misalignment after roll change.
- Loose chock clamp or damaged bearing seal.
- Process instability from speed mismatch or uneven cooling.

Checks:
- Verify vibration trend at motor and drive-side bearing.
- Inspect lubrication flow and oil pressure.
- Check chock clamp torque and roll alignment.
- Compare roll gap deviation with strip speed and temperature trend.

Immediate actions:
- Reduce mill speed if vibration exceeds alarm limit.
- Confirm lubrication flow before continuing production.
- Schedule bearing inspection when vibration is repeated across two coils.
- Stop the stand if bearing temperature and vibration both breach limits.

## HRM-FURN-02 reheating furnace walking beam

Criticality: high

Common symptoms:
- Slab transfer delay.
- Hydraulic pressure instability.
- Uneven furnace zone temperature.
- Walking beam position error.

Likely causes:
- Hydraulic actuator leakage.
- Scale buildup around skid or beam guides.
- Furnace zone burner imbalance.
- Position sensor drift.

Checks:
- Compare hydraulic pressure with walking beam travel time.
- Inspect skid/beam guide for mechanical obstruction.
- Review burner zone temperature deviation.
- Validate position sensor feedback against manual reference.

Immediate actions:
- Isolate hydraulic circuit if pressure drop is severe.
- Clear scale buildup during the next planned window.
- Recalibrate position sensor after mechanical inspection.
- Keep spare seal kit ready when pressure instability repeats.

## HRM-COIL-03 down coiler mandrel

Criticality: critical

Common symptoms:
- Coil tail-end slip.
- Mandrel expansion fault.
- High motor current during wrapping.
- Telescopic coil formation.

Likely causes:
- Mandrel segment wear.
- Hydraulic expansion pressure loss.
- Wrapper roll misalignment.
- Control timing mismatch at tail end.

Checks:
- Verify mandrel hydraulic pressure during expansion.
- Inspect mandrel segment surface condition.
- Check wrapper roll alignment and actuation timing.
- Compare motor current trend with coil diameter.

Immediate actions:
- Hold production if expansion failure repeats.
- Inspect hydraulic valve and seal pack.
- Align wrapper roll before the next campaign.
- Prioritize mandrel segment procurement when lead time is high.
